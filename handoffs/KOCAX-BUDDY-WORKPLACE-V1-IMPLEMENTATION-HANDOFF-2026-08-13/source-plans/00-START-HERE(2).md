The audience/product split is frozen. The marks are not. `KocaX Operations` is the selected working B2B name, not a legal availability conclusion; public release must use the cleared name or an explicitly approved fallback. Use the full `KocaX` form in working materials; do not rely on `KX Operations` alone because `KX` is crowded in business and technology contexts.

## The core separation

### KocaX Buddy

Personal, warm and character-led:

- one identity across devices and approved channels;
- appearance, clothes, palette, voice and conversation style;
- everyday planning, reminders, writing, files and organization;
- Memory Vault, permissions, approvals, receipts, pause, export and deletion;
- KocaX Messenger as the native conversation channel;
- Buddy Workplace as the personal control panel;
- €19.99/month only if the final usage allowance supports sustainable unit economics;
- adult users first; no business CRM, staff, quote or accounting positioning.

### KocaX Operations

Professional, operational and outcome-led:

- intake, lead follow-up, planning, service, documents and reporting;
- workflow engine, integrations, policy controls, approvals, audit and monitoring;
- Operations Console rather than an avatar or personal character;
- implementation from €4,999 excluding VAT, or managed delivery from €500/month excluding VAT with a defined scope and initial term;
- KocaX Business OS only where the implementation needs the full workspace;
- no clothing, companion, friendship, personality or “your company Buddy” language.

2. Run brand clearance before buying campaigns, filing marks or commissioning large rebrand work.
3. Build and verify the new pages in a protected preview.
4. Build Personal Buddy's real product controls before publishing strong memory/action claims.
5. Move the business content and navigation atomically with route, SEO and redirect changes.
6. Complete privacy, consumer, security and IP gates before taking Personal Buddy subscriptions.
7. Release Personal Buddy and KocaX Operations independently; one must not block or misrepresent the other.

## Immediate no-go rules

- No business offer may use Buddy as its name, mascot or primary interface identity.
- No public page may call MATE or “Buddyfather” the customer's assistant.
- No page may present a scripted screen as live automation.
- No feature may be claimed as available without release evidence.
- No subscription may go live without self-service cancellation and the applicable withdrawal flow.
- No consequential action may bypass the permission and approval policy.
- No claim of end-to-end encryption, fully local processing, zero-access, no training, full deletion or 24/7 availability may appear without proof for the complete path.

## Recommended implementation order

5. Reposition Business OS beneath Operations.

## Definition of success

A new visitor should immediately understand:

- **Buddy is for me personally.**
- **KocaX Operations is for my company.**
- **KocaX Business OS is a professional workspace, not another Buddy.**
- **KocaX Messenger is where I talk to my personal Buddy.**
- **Buddy Workplace is where I control my personal Buddy.**
- **The Operations Console is where a company controls workflows.**

If a visitor can still confuse personal Buddy with company automation, the separation is not finished.
